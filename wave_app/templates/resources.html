Resources for Facilitators

What can you use this application for?

This application is designed to give facilitators of all kinds of events, workshops, classes, talks, and presentations an easy and engaging way to break the ice and encourage participation.

Participants can anonymously send a message to another participant and receive one in return. You can also use the app to ask your audience for anonymous feedback, thoughts, questions, or reactions during your session — with their responses displayed directly on your screen.

This gives you a simple way to get a sense of how your participants are doing, what they think about a particular topic, or whether they have questions, without putting anyone on the spot.

Our goal is to make sessions feel more relaxed, interactive, and inclusive — and to make it easier for people to share their thoughts and ideas.

What is the “Bottle Exchange” function?

With Bottle Exchange, you create a prompt that participants can access easily using a QR code. You can ask them to share their thoughts on a topic, answer a question, or respond to something relevant to your session.

Once a participant submits their response — their “bottle” — it is anonymously passed to another random participant in the session, or what we call a wave.

The idea is simple and fun:

“I put something into the room anonymously, and I receive something from someone else in the group.”

This creates a playful way for participants to connect with each other while keeping everyone anonymous.

What is the “Pulse Check Bottle” function?

The Pulse Check Bottle lets you check in with your audience at any point during your event.

Simply press “Enable Pulse Check” and participants will receive a free-text question where they can share their thoughts anonymously.

You might ask:

  • Are there any questions that haven't been answered?
  • What do you think about this key topic?
  • Is anything unclear?
  • How are you feeling about the session so far?
  • Is there anything you'd like to share?

Their responses are sent directly to your screen, giving you an instant sense of what your audience is thinking or feeling — without requiring anyone to speak up in front of the group.

What is the biggest benefit for participants?

Anonymity.

You can reassure participants that when they join your session (your wave), their messages remain completely anonymous. No name or email address is attached to the bottled messages they submit.

This can make it much easier for people to share honest thoughts, questions, feelings, or ideas — particularly when they might otherwise hesitate to speak up.

What is our most important advice?

Make sure participants understand the difference between joining anonymously and choosing to provide their email address.

When participants join your wave through the QR code, entering an email address is completely optional. If they choose to provide one, it allows you to follow up with them after the session — for example, to share your presentation slides or additional materials.

If they don't provide an email address, they can still participate anonymously.

Our recommendation: make this clear when introducing the application, so participants know exactly what information they are sharing and why.
