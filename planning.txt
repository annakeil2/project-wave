Message in a Bottle App 
Aim is to provide facilitators of all kinds of events, such as workshops, classes, speeches, an easy and gamified way to break the ice and make participants add as well as 
receive an anonymous message from another participant in the session. The recipient does not see who sent the Bottle.

# Users
Superuser
Facilitator
Participants

# Session 
Wave: PulseCheck_Enabled, Bottles_Enabled
Bottles
Release

## Types of Interaction via bottle messages
# Icebreaker
Facilitator creates prompt before a jamboree and participants can add their messages that are sent to another particant radomly, in an anomymous way.
# PulseCheck
When the facilitator enables this feature, participants can send input that is directly sent to main screen.
# FollowUp
Facilitator can opt for sending input form to all participants who signed up with their email address.
